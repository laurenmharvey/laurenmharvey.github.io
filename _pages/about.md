I am a Ph.D. candidate in sociology at Duke University. My research focuses on race, ethnicity, and inequality, with particular interests in group boundaries, digital racial studies, and identity performance.

My current work examines how people perform race and evaluate cultural boundary-crossing on TikTok. At Duke, I am also pursuing certificates in African and African American Studies and College Teaching.
